Funds for Caney Creek NJROTC cadets.

The booster club raises money for the Naval Junior Reserve Officer Training Corps at Caney Creek High School. Parents, neighbors, and local businesses keep the unit’s travel, meets, and events going.

Saturday, November 21, 2026 · 8:00 AM to noon Central

Caney Creek High School

13470 FM 1485, Caney Creek, TX 77306

Cars are $35 and limited to 100. Vendor booths are $20. Spectators are welcome. Proceeds support the booster club and the Grangerland Intermediate PTO.
