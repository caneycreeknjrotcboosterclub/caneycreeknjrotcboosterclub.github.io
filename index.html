Funds for Caney Creek NJROTC cadets.

The booster club raises money for the Naval Junior Reserve Officer Training Corps at Caney Creek High School. Parents, neighbors, and local businesses keep the unit’s travel, meets, and events going.

Fall Car Show

Saturday, November 21, 2026 · 8:00 AM–noon Central

Caney Creek High School

13470 FM 1485, Caney Creek, TX 77306

Show your ride, look at the cars, and support cadets at Caney Creek and students at Grangerland Intermediate. Presented with the Grangerland Intermediate PTO. Spectators are welcome. Jeremiah’s Italian Ice will be on site. Trophies include Cadets’ Choice, Grangerland Panthers Choice, and People’s Choice.

Show cars are limited to 100. Pre-registration is recommended.
